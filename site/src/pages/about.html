nexlibrOS is a personal fork of the excellent CrossPoint Reader, the open firmware for Xteink e-readers, tuned for the touchscreen X4 Pro. It keeps everything CrossPoint does and adds a few quality-of-life features: small Lua apps on the SD card, a library organised by series, tags and custom lists, long-press dictionary lookups and highlights, a simplified settings screen, and a quiet home screen built around the books you are reading.

The rest of this page is the user guide, which also ships on the demo SD card as an EPUB.
